# PinoyBuyNSell promotional video exports

Created October 12, 2026. The 20-second videos use the site's existing brand mark and actual administrator listing photos: Kobe Bryant (17), Michael Jordan (18) and Mewtwo (21). All three listings were active when their public photos were retrieved. The photos retain their correct EXIF orientation and are fitted whole into the video layout. Captions contain no prices, follower claims, customer testimonials or guaranteed availability.

- `pinoybuynsell-facebook.mp4`: 1080 × 1920, 25 fps; suited to Facebook Reels.
- `pinoybuynsell-website.mp4`: 1920 × 1080, 25 fps; suited to the website and landscape posts.
- Both use H.264, yuv420p, AAC stereo and an MP4 fast-start header.
- Music is an original instrumental generated from oscillators and percussion noise for this video. No external music recording or voice recording is used.
- The five scenes are the opening, Kobe, Jordan, Mewtwo and the website/follow call to action. Scenes have gentle motion and short crossfades; captions remain readable without audio.

After website deployment, `/marketing/index.html` provides previews, same-origin MP4 download buttons and a suggested caption. The preview page uses `preload="none"`, has no autoplay, has no external scripts and is marked noindex. This is a standalone asset page. It sends no Facebook post and needs no new environment variable or database migration.
